    • Gantry radarLive

    Calls and enforcement

    Through AstraAlert and Central Plexus

    • Emergency call boxesLive

      Calls reach the control room over SIP, each tied to its box.

    • Call box healthLive

      Status messages from each box update its power, battery, solar and door state.

    • AI voice BeingLive

      Off by default. Answers in Hindi when no operator picks up, then files an incident with a briefing.
